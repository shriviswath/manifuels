⚙ TANK SETTINGS
Tolerance is the swing you accept as normal measurement error and evaporation. Anything past it is flagged. Typical starting point: 0.3% of a full tanker, so 20–30 L.

Dashed line is the reserve you never want to go below. Where a tank line crosses it is the runout date. This is arithmetic on your own shift register, not a prediction — it assumes trade continues as it has been.
⚙ ORDERING RULES
Reserve is the volume you never sell — dead stock below the suction pipe plus whatever margin you want. Runout is measured to this, not to zero.
Supplier minimum is the load size they insist on when they will not send a normal one. It only matters if it still fits in the tank.
